## How To Test The Companion
### Two links, two very different roles

- **Speaker link** carries a session token, drives the deck, and shows these notes
- **Audience link** carries no token and can only vote and ask
- Advance a slide from your phone; this projector should follow immediately
- Try the audience link on a second device to see both sides at once
