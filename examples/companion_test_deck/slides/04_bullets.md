## What The Remote Controls
### Bullet list layout

- **Next / Previous** advance the deck from your pocket
- **Blackout (B)** and **Whiteout (W)** kill the screen mid-question
- **Timer** starts, pauses, and resets the talk clock
- Speaker notes stream to the phone and never to the audience
- Live poll tallies update on the projector as votes land
