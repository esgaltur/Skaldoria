## Pacing Maths
### Math formula layout

$$ \Delta t = t_{elapsed} - \left( \frac{T_{target}}{N_{total}} \right) \cdot i_{current} $$

- A positive delta means you are running behind
- The speaker phone shows the same clock as the console
