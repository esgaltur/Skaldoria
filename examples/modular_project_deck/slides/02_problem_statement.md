## The Monolithic Bottleneck

- **Context Fragmentation**: 5,000-line documents create merge conflicts across engineering teams
- **Slow Feedback Loops**: Single edits require re-scanning the entire monolithic codebase
- **Zero Reusability**: Sections cannot be shared or composed across different presentations
- **The Modular Paradigm**: Independent slide files coordinated by a clean project manifest
